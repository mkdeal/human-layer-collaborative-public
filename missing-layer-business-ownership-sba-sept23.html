Call to Action

The Exercise

  • Name one thing eating your attention
  • Which ring is it in?
  • If it is in release or monitor, what sits nearby in influence or control?

A big-box competitor opening nearby sits in Monitor. What sits in Control is the reason your regulars choose you: your hours, your greeting, and the follow-up call nobody else makes.

Stephen Covey, The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People (1989), built on the Stoic dichotomy of control from Epictetus

The parting practice  ·  Box Breath
  • Inhale for 4, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold for 4
